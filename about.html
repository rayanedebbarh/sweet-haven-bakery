Our Story

Baking happiness into every pastry since 2020

Welcome to Sweet Haven

Sweet Haven Bakery was born from a simple dream: to bring the authentic taste of artisan pastries to the heart of Alma, Michigan. Founded in 2020, we've been dedicated to crafting exceptional baked goods that bring joy to our community.

Every morning before sunrise, our skilled bakers begin their work, mixing dough, folding butter into delicate layers, and filling the air with the irresistible aroma of fresh pastries. We believe that the best baked goods start with the finest ingredients, which is why we partner with local Michigan farms for our flour, butter, eggs, and seasonal produce.

Our Philosophy

At Sweet Haven, we believe baking is both an art and a science. Each pastry that leaves our kitchen represents hours of training, years of experience, and a genuine passion for the craft. We don't take shortcuts, and we never compromise on quality.

From our classic French croissants to our innovative seasonal creations, every item on our menu is made from scratch using traditional techniques passed down through generations of bakers. We're proud to serve our community with products we would be honored to share with our own families.

Community Commitment

We're more than just a bakery – we're a proud member of the Alma community. We regularly donate pastries to local schools, support community events, and provide internship opportunities for aspiring bakers. Your support helps us give back to the place we call home.
